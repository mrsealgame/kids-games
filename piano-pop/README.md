# 🎹 Piano Pop!

A fun piano sight-reading game for kids! 🎵

- 🎼 Practice reading musical notes and playing piano.
- 🎵 Play classic songs or try randomly generated Mystery Melodies.
- 🌱 Easy / ⭐ Normal / 😈 Devil difficulty levels.
- 🏆 Earn rewards and track your best scores.

**Read the notes. Play the piano. Pop the stars!** ⭐
